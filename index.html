VIEW THE ROLL →

Every event ends. The frame doesn't. Tasveer is IIIT Delhi's media society — the crew that shows up with cameras when the campus makes a memory, and makes sure it outlives the moment.

We shoot, film, design and archive. Not for the feed — for the record. The campus's story, told by the people who live it.

01SHOOT

Cameras up at every fest, talk and late-night jam, from the front row to the rafters.

02FILM

Aftermovies, reels and drone passes over the backyard, cut while the night is still warm.

03DESIGN

Posters, covers and the look of every event, drawn before the first camera comes out.

04ARCHIVE

Every frame kept, named and filed, so the campus can look back at what it was.
